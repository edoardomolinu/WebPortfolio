Designed to be Bold

Hi, I am Edoardo Molinu. I am an Industrial Designer focused on turning technical complexity into products where aesthetics and function meet. I shape meaningful user experiences, engineered from the start for real-world industrial production.

Leo | Casa Bugatti

A toaster that transcends the role of a simple appliance into a collectible piece of art, transforming the bread-toasting ritual into an iconic experience tailored for modern luxury micro-living.

10EXT | Politecnico di Milano

An automated industrial machine engineered to close the loop on polymer recycling for small businesses, producing high-precision, production-ready spools for 3D printing.

LuminAir | Midea Group

An active hygiene module developed for next-generation dishwashers, harnessing catalytic reactions to eliminate odors and continuously sanitize appliance cavities between wash cycles.

Climbex | Politecnico di Milano

Biomimetic alpine footwear designed through nature-inspired biomechanics, engineered to redefine grip, adaptability, and performance across extreme rock and mountain terrains.
