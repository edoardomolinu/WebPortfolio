Climbex | Politecnico di Milano

Biomimicry is the practice of studying nature's designs, processes, and strategies to solve human problems. It involves observing how organisms have adapted over time and applying those insights to innovate and improve our own technologies and systems.

The theme of our exploration lies hidden among the mountain ranges. It is here that we rediscover a long-lost connection to nature, attempting to close the gap by observing and mimicking the behaviors of the wildlife that calls it home.

Our project is directed to the closely knit community of climbers and outdoor enthusiasts seeking performance through bio-inspired innovation and a Deep habitat reconnection.

To solve these challenges, we took a step back to look at nature's proven designs and seek inspiration in the ibex, master of climbing.

Years of evolution have given them outstanding qualities and adaptations to best survive in rocky environments to find refuge.

Rigid outer edge

A hard, sharp keratin rim that acts as a micro-support, channelling weight onto millimetric rock crevices without deforming.

Soft central pad

An elastic, rubbery inner cushion which deforms to mirror the rough texture of stone, creating a high-friction suction effect while acting as a natural shock absorber.

Shore A 65 rubber

  • High-friction suction effect
    for slabs & overhangs
  • Shock absorption
  • Increased sensitivity

Shore A 85 rubber

  • Edge stability
  • Power transfer
  • Grip on small holds
  • Durability

Climbex combines a fast, reinforced biomimetic velcro strap, pull loops for easy slip-on and transport, an asymmetric tongue for a precise fit, and a Sling shot structure for support, shape retention, and power transfer.
