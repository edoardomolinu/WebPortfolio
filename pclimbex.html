Climbex | PoliMi

The climbing shoe is a highly technological object that challenges limits to achieve the best performance. The goal of the Climbex project is to go even deeper through a biomimetic approach. Thus, by observing how the ibex hoof has adapted over time to the rock, the project found the intuition to improve existing technologies.

The theme of our exploration lies hidden among the mountain ranges. It is here that we rediscover a long-lost connection to nature, attempting to close the gap by observing and mimicking the behaviors of the wildlife that calls it home.

Rigid outer edge

A hard, sharp keratin rim that acts as a micro-support, channelling weight onto millimetric rock crevices without deforming.

Soft central pad

An elastic, rubbery inner cushion which deforms to mirror the rough texture of stone, creating a high-friction suction effect while acting as a natural shock absorber.

Shore A 65 rubber

  • High-friction suction effect
    for slabs & overhangs
  • Shock absorption
  • Increased sensitivity

Shore A 85 rubber

  • Edge stability
  • Power transfer
  • Grip on small holds
  • Durability

Down Turn

Climbex combines a downturn transferring power for stability, an asymmetric shape increasing tension for big toe precision, a dedicated toe patch for hooking, and a toe cap shielding toes against impacts and heavy abrasion.

Closing system

Climbex combines a fast, reinforced biomimetic velcro strap, pull loops for easy slip-on and transport, an asymmetric tongue for a precise fit, and a Sling shot structure for support, shape retention, and power transfer.

Design Journey

Completed in just one week, Climbex merged traditional design methods with early AI tools. The workflow evolved into three phases: paper sketching, digital transposition, and Vizcom prompting for the final 3D rendering.
